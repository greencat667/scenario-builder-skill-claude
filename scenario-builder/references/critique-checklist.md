# Scenario critique checklist

Run every check against the draft worlds before showing them. Rewrite anything that fails, and record what you changed in the report's "Critique notes" appendix.

## The set as a whole

- [ ] **No heaven/hell/muddle.** Ask: "Which world would the commissioning team vote for?" If the answer is obvious, reframe the axes or rebalance the worlds until each has real upsides and real costs.
- [ ] **No probabilities.** No world is labelled "most likely", "base case", "business as usual" or "expected".
- [ ] **The official future is in there — and challenged.** One world may resemble what the team already expects, but at least one other must break a comfortable assumption.
- [ ] **Distinct enough to change a decision.** For each pair of worlds, name one decision that would come out differently. If you can't, merge or rebuild.
- [ ] **Independent axes.** Would a sceptic find all four quadrants plausible? A quadrant that needs a contradiction to exist (e.g. "booming funding" + "councils abolished") means the axes are correlated.
- [ ] **Predetermined elements everywhere.** Every predetermined element appears, or is at least not contradicted, in every world.
- [ ] **The user's organisation isn't an axis.** The worlds are defined by forces outside its control.

## Each world

- [ ] **Causal, not magical.** The "How we got here" story has turning points that follow from drivers. No "then suddenly everyone agreed".
- [ ] **Consistent with its poles.** Re-read the two poles and check every bullet against them.
- [ ] **Winners *and* losers.** Named groups, not "some people".
- [ ] **A daily reality, not a trend list.** "What's true" bullets describe what life is like, not what's "increasing".
- [ ] **Vignette is ordinary.** One fictional person, an ordinary day, a small tension, concrete detail. No speeches about the future. No real private individuals. No real small place or organisation used in a way that reads as a claim about it.
- [ ] **Memorable name.** Could someone use it in a meeting without explaining it?

## Evidence

- [ ] **Every present-day fact is cited** with a working link. No invented statistics.
- [ ] **Future claims trace to a driver.** If a world depends on something not in the driver inventory, add the driver (with evidence) or cut the claim.
- [ ] **Thin evidence is flagged,** not hidden.

## Indicators and moves

- [ ] **Indicators discriminate.** Each fires more in its world than in the others. Delete any that would fire everywhere.
- [ ] **Indicators have a threshold and a source.**
- [ ] **Forecastable questions are resolvable** — a specific event, a date, and a source that will settle it.
- [ ] **No-regret moves really are.** For each, name the world where it's weakest and check it still helps there.
- [ ] **Big bets name their trigger** — the indicator that would justify making them.

## Style

- [ ] Plain English, short sentences, no consultant-speak ("stakeholder ecosystem", "paradigm shift", "leverage synergies").
- [ ] No meta-text, word counts or drafting notes in the output.
