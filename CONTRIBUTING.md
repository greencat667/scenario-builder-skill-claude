# Contributing to Scenario Builder

Thanks for your interest in improving this.

**This repo is not actively maintained.** It was published as a one-time release, not something
I'm committed to reviewing or updating on an ongoing basis. Issues and PRs may sit unanswered for
a long time, or indefinitely. If you need changes, forking is likely faster than waiting on a
response here.

## What's most useful

- Improvements to the axis-selection and independence checks
- Better early-warning indicator guidance, especially for linking to signal monitoring and forecasting
- Worked examples in other domains
- An eval suite (none exists yet)

## How to contribute

1. Fork the repository
2. Create a branch (`git checkout -b improvement/your-change`)
3. Make your changes
4. Open a pull request with a clear description of what you changed and why

## What to avoid

- Changes specific to one organisation's branding or workflow — keep it general
- Real internal strategy material as examples — keep worked examples fictional or clearly yours to publish
- Adding probabilities to scenarios — the method deliberately avoids them

## Questions?

Open an issue if you like, but see the note above — don't expect a quick reply.
